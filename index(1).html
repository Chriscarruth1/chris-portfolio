About Me

I tell community-centered stories that reveal the people behind the work. My experience in field communications and operational culture building has shaped a narrative style rooted in connection, belonging, and place. I look for the moments that show who we are—the moments that deserve to be amplified, honored, and shared. My work centers on building trust, strengthening local relationships, and ensuring the story told is authentic to the community it reflects.

Featured Projects

PPE Recycling & Sustainability Partnership Program

This project began with a simple observation: PPE that had served its purpose still had value beyond disposal. I helped launch a sustainability partnership with Soles4Souls to collect and repurpose gently used safety vests and gloves. The initiative not only diverted waste from landfills, but also connected our site to communities in need. What could have been trash became support, dignity, and continued purpose.

M.A.D.E. at Amazon Associate Recognition Program

The MADE program was created to spotlight the everyday leadership that already existed on the floor. I developed a layered recognition approach that honored gratitude, mutual respect, and peer-to-peer appreciation. From casual shout-outs to monthly highlights and a site-wide Wall of Fame, the program helped associates see themselves as leaders and culture shapers—not just workers clocking in.

Local Media & Community Relationship Building

Building trust starts long before a story runs. I’ve developed relationships with local reporters, community partners, and civic voices by showing up consistently—at community events, leadership roundtables, and neighborhood conversations. My focus is on listening first, understanding what matters most locally, and telling stories that reflect those shared priorities. This work has strengthened positive perception of the site and deepened Amazon’s presence within the community.
